Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Survivlo ("the app", "we", "us") collects when you play, and how it is handled. The short version: the app collects nothing. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

1. There Is No Account

Survivlo has no sign-in, no user profile and no server of its own. You never give us your name, email address or any other personal information to play.

2. No Data Collection

The app does not collect, send, share or sell any data about you. It has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party service that collects data. The app does not connect to the internet at all while you play.

This matches the declaration the app makes to Apple under "App Privacy": Data Not Collected.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device

So that you can continue where you stopped, the app keeps a few things in on-device storage:

  • your saved game (the scene you reached and the choices the story remembers);
  • the story points you have found and how many times you died (shown in STORYLINE);
  • the sound setting;
  • whether the app has already asked you for a rating.

This data never leaves your device and we never see it.

4. Ratings and Reviews

Once, after the first test of the story, the app may show Apple's standard rating window. Whether it appears is decided by Apple, and whatever you choose or write there is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. The app never sees your rating or your review.

5. Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Photos

Survivlo never requests access to any of these. It is a story game played entirely with taps.

6. Children's Privacy

Survivlo is rated 13+ and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Everything listed in Section 3 stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. NEW GAME clears your saved game. Deleting the app removes all of it.

8. Your Rights

Because we hold no account and no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete: deleting the app erases everything. If you have a question about privacy, including requests under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA, contact us at the address below and we will help. EEA/UK users may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

9. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not have any.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published here with a new "Last updated" date. If the app ever starts collecting any data, this policy and the App Store privacy declaration will be updated first.
