How do I start the story again?

In the menu tap NEW GAME. Your saved game is replaced, but everything you discovered stays in STORYLINE.

What is STORYLINE?

It shows how many story points of the scenario you have found, how many times you died, which ways to die you have discovered and which endings you have reached. Story points you have not reached yet stay hidden, so it never spoils the story.

I died. Did I lose everything?

No. Every death explains what went wrong and sends you back to the start of the current test, with your earlier choices kept.

Is my progress backed up?

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, it is gone. Survivlo has no account and no server.

Are the survival facts real?

Yes, they are based on real facts, but they are simplified for the story. They are not safety or medical advice; see the Terms of Service.

Something's not working / I found a bug

Email kajopn8n@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and where in the story it happened. Screenshots help a lot.
